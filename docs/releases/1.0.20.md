Smooth effects at high frame rates: in a render faster than the game's ticks, slowed down, the blood and the other effects no longer hold their picture for several frames while they fly on.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.20_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

## Effects at high frame rates

- CS2 draws blood, fire, explosions, muzzle flashes and impact dust with sheets of pictures played over each particle's life. OFAST showed one whole picture at a time, so a blood splash, 16 pictures over half a second, changed its picture about 30 times a second whatever the render's frame rate: in a render at 250 frames a second slowed down, the blood held each picture for several frames while it moved on.
- Each picture now fades into the next one, as CS2 draws them: the blood (its splashes, streaks and mists), the fires, the HE blasts, the muzzle flashes and the impact dust change at every frame, at any frame rate.
- Checked on an AWP hit of a round on Mirage, filmed every quarter of a tick (about 250 frames a second): before, the blood's streak kept its shape, then jumped; now it changes as much at every frame.
- Where two pictures overlap the effects look a touch softer than before, as in CS2.

Send a round again to get it. The rounds already in Unreal get it at the same time: they share the effects' material.

## Known issues

- The snipers' flash seen from another player (third person) was not compared with CS2: it is now a full fireball at the barrel for 2 – 3 ticks.
- CS2's bodies crumple at the knees from the first instants; OFAST's start folding a little later.
- A player killed while crouching straightens their legs a little before the body falls.
- With the players layer, the players are lit without the light bounced from the walls around them: in a dark place they come out too dark.
- A matchmaking or tournament demo recorded since CS2's update of November 21 has not been tried yet: if one does not open, please report it on GitHub.

Checked on CS2 1.42.0.0 (build 2000948).
