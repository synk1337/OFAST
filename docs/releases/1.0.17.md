Four fixes from a round recorded on Mirage: the view through a sniper's scope, the blur, the USP-S's flash and how the dead fall.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.17_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

## Through a sniper's scope (realistic look)

- The view through an AWP's, SSG 08's, SCAR-20's or G3SG1's scope came out blown white: the camera's exposure took the scope's black ring, two thirds of the picture, for a dark scene and brightened everything. While the scope is up, the exposure now leaves the ring out, so the view through it is about as bright as without it.
- Unticking **Scope overlay** in the Look panel turns this off along with the ring.

## Blur

- Through the players' eyes the blur is now **Blur with distance** by default: the gun and what is near stay sharp, and the map blurs more the further away it is, like a depth pass in After Effects. It starts lighter: **Depth of field** 0.5, 0.4 % of the picture's width far away.
- A scope's zoom no longer blurs the view far more than the eyes' own view.
- Maps you already sent in the realistic look take the new default the next time you open them, unless you had changed their blur.
- Untick **Blur with distance** to get the lens focused where the player looks, as before.

## USP-S

- A silenced USP-S's flash now starts at the silencer's tip, as CS2 draws it, instead of inside the silencer, in first and third person.

## Deaths

- Bodies fell harder than in CS2. When a player's pose moved in their last instant, OFAST took that motion over a single tick, so a small jump of the pose threw the body; it is now taken over a tenth of a second. The hit's push is also halved: an AWP shot flipped standing bodies over backward, legs up.
- Compared with CS2's own playback of five kills of one round (three AWP, two USP-S), filmed from the same places at the same moments.

Rounds already sent keep their old version: send a round again to get the fixes.

## Known issues

- CS2's bodies still fold at the knees and drop where they stand sooner than OFAST's.
- With the players layer, the players are lit without the light bounced from the walls around them: in a dark place they come out too dark.
- Unreal sometimes stopped with a graphics card error while loading the realistic look of Mirage in our tests; it is being looked at.
- A matchmaking or tournament demo recorded since CS2's update of November 21 has not been tried yet: if one does not open, please report it on GitHub.

Checked on CS2 1.42.0.0 (build 2000948).
