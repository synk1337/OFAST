The first release of OFAST: a CS2 demo's round rebuilt in Unreal Engine 5.8 in a few clicks, from your own CS2 files.

**Download** `OFAST.Studio_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it (Windows 10 or 11; it installs for your user only). You also need Counter-Strike 2 and Unreal Engine 5.8 (free, from the Epic Games Launcher). The setup program is not signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher (**More info → Run anyway**). How to use it, step by step: the [user guide](https://github.com/synk1337/OFAST/blob/main/docs/user-guide/README.md).

## What is in 1.0

- **OFAST Studio**: your CS2 demos, each match read in seconds (rounds, scores, kill feed), a finder for the moments worth filming (multi-kills, clutches, collaterals, no-scopes, wallbangs, through smoke, airborne, blind, knife), a 2D replay on the radar, a camera per kill, **Send to Unreal**, **Open in Unreal** and **Render**; a check of your setup, CS2's version included.
- **Maps** from CS2's own files, with CS2's materials, lights, sky and tone curve, checked against CS2 screenshots.
- **Players**: agents and gloves animated by CS2's own animation graphs and the poses the demo carries, ragdoll deaths, each player's first-person view with their viewmodel, FOV, offsets, hand and recoil.
- **Skins**: paint kits in all nine of CS2's paint styles with wear and pattern seed, glove skins, pearlescence, stickers (on the model's places, freely placed ones too), swinging charms, name tags, counting StatTrak modules.
- **Grenades and shots**: smokes, molotovs, HE blasts, flashes; every bullet with its tracer, muzzle flash, impact, blood, bullet hole and case, through walls as in CS2.
- **Cameras**: players' eyes (as played or smoothed), shot cameras around each kill, HLAE camera paths in and out.
- **Renders**: Movie Render Queue presets (preview, final with compositing passes, 4K) into `Videos\OFAST`.
- **`ofast-tools` zip**: the `ofast` command line, and `ofast-attach` with its DLL to play OFAST's cameras in a CS2 started with `-insecure` and record the frames.

Checked on CS2 1.41.9.3 (build 2000938). The Studio shows your CS2 version in orange when CS2 has updated since.

## Known limits

- Glitter and iridescent skins show without their sparkle; a few rare map shaders (water, some liquids) are basic.
- Importing a round takes Unreal 15–20 GB of RAM; the first round on a map also imports the map and compiles its shaders (a while, once per map).
- Windows only.

OFAST never ships Valve content: everything comes from your own CS2 install, for your own videos.
