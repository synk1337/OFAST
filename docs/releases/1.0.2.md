Effects much closer to CS2's, and a round seen through one player's eyes from start to end.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.2_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version (no need to uninstall first): the maps and rounds you already sent stay. Send a round again to get the new effects and cameras in it.

## A round about one player

- **Point of view**, above the round's kill feed in the Studio, picks whose round it is: the round is seen through their eyes from its start to 2 seconds after their death (to its end if they live), and Unreal's sequence stops there. Switching between players ruined a round; now only that player's kills make cuts (to the kill camera you picked for each). OFAST picks the player of the highlight you clicked in the finder, else the round's top fragger. **Each kill's killer** keeps the old cutting from killer to killer. On the command line: `ofast export-demo --pov <Steam id or name>`.

## Effects rebuilt for more fidelity

Rebuilt from CS2's own particle files and compared frame by frame with CS2 playing the same demo.

- **Molotovs and incendiaries**: the burst they light with, thrown the way the bottle flew (a fireball turning to black smoke, burning splashes; an incendiary's sparks), read from the demo itself; then a dense carpet of flames in CS2's colours (cream and peach where they overlap), a white-hot heart for the first seconds, and the magenta-fringed flames along the edge you see.
- **HE grenades**: a bigger orange fireball over its own dark smoke, jets of fire, sparks and flecks.
- **Flashbangs**: a soft white glow that peaks a few frames in and fades, as CS2's. Before, a hard white disc floated above it and its light blew the whole room out white.
- **Smokes**: shaded in every crease and on their side away from the light, grey, with puffy billows that roll upward; they fade out thinning instead of breaking into holes.
- **Blood**: each hit's blood sized as CS2 sizes it, by the damage it did: splashes on the player hit, dark streaks flying off, a red haze; sparks when a helmet takes a headshot.
