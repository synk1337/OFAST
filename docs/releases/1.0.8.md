A new look for the Studio, and a demo list that keeps up with your folder.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.8_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

## New icon and logo

The Studio has its own icon, an orange O with a wedge in its corner, and the OFAST logo in its header.

## Demos show up by themselves

Demos you download from CS2's Watch tab or copy into the folder while the Studio is open now appear in the list within a few seconds, without restarting it. Deleted ones go away.

Checked on CS2 1.41.9.5 (build 2000940).
