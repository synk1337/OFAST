A realistic look for your rounds, blood in one colour, guns that stay out of walls, and updates from inside the Studio.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.3_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version (no need to uninstall first): the maps and rounds you already sent stay. Send a round again to get the changes in it.

## Updates from the Studio

This is the last version you install by hand. From now on, when the Studio starts it checks GitHub for a newer OFAST: a bar under its title then says which version is out, with **What's new** and **Update**. Update downloads it, checks it was signed by OFAST's own release, installs it and restarts the Studio (not while a round is being sent or rendered). **Later** hides the bar until the next start.

## A realistic look

The select beside **Send to Unreal** now picks the map's look:

- **CS2's look**: the picture CS2 shows, as before.
- **Realistic**: the map lit like the real world (a real sun and sky, real light levels, a camera's exposure), with an ambiance: a warm haze that the sun's beams show in through doorways and roofs, light shafts round the sun, a little film grain, and depth of field on the players' eyes, which focus on what they look at. Smokes, fires, flashes and blood are lit to match.

The first round you send in the realistic look on a map imports that map again in it (a while, once per map). After that rounds take as long as in CS2's look. The Studio remembers the look you picked.

## Fixed

- **Guns in walls**: in a player's point of view, long guns (AWP, M4s) no longer sink into a wall the player stands against. The gun is drawn the way CS2 draws it, always in front of the world.
- **Blood in one colour**: the splash on the player hit was a bright pink-red beside dark red streaks. Blood is now lit by the scene as in CS2: one red, warm in the sun, darker in the shade.
- **Bullet impacts**: the dust and smoke where bullets hit no longer glow in the shade (most visible in the realistic look).
- **Update cameras** keeps the realistic look's depth of field.

Checked on CS2 1.41.9.4 (build 2000939).
