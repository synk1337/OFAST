Seen through a player's eyes, a slow reload now lasts as long as it does in CS2.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.16_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

## Slow reloads in first person

- In CS2 a reload started while holding the walk key is a slow reload: it takes twice as long as a normal one. OFAST played its animation twice as fast, so the gun was back at rest halfway through and stayed still for about two seconds while the player was still reloading. The animation now ends when CS2's reload ends.
- Compared with CS2's own playback of two slow reloads of a tournament demo (an AK-47 and a Galil AR, FaZe vs Falcons on Anubis), and with reloads recorded in a local game on CS2 1.42.0.0 (AK-47, AWP, Desert Eagle, Nova).
- Rounds without a slow reload export exactly as before.
- Rounds already sent keep their old animation: send a round again to get the fix.

## Known issues

- With the players layer, the players are lit without the light bounced from the walls around them: in a dark place they come out too dark.
- A matchmaking or tournament demo recorded since CS2's update of November 21 has not been tried yet: if one does not open, please report it on GitHub.

Checked on CS2 1.42.0.0 (build 2000948).
