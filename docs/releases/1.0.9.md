Smokes and molotovs that look like CS2's, feet on the stairs, a depth of field that blurs the map, and CS2's own poses back on new demos.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.9_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

**Send your rounds again** to see the new smokes, molotovs, feet and poses. The first round you send on each map imports that map again (a few minutes, once per map and look), so its glass is drawn under the smokes.

## Smokes

- Smokes are no longer see-through: you can't see players through a full smoke, and window glass doesn't show through it.
- They reach the walls and floors they lie against, as in CS2: no more gap beside a smoke in a passage or doorway.
- They grow out of the grenade over their first second (first puff at about half a second), where the whole cloud used to appear at once.
- Sharper puffs and shaded creases, without grain in renders, and in CS2's colours: grey for the CTs, tan-brown for the Ts, whatever the map's sun.

## Molotovs

A molotov dies down over its last second instead of switching off, its light with it, and its last flames stay small, bright and orange to the end, not purple wisps.

## Feet on stairs and slopes

Players' feet stand on the steps and slopes under them, where they used to float over the lower steps and sink into the uphill side. The body comes down only as far as the legs can't reach, and the demo's hits still land on the bodies.

## Depth of field and field of view

- **Depth of field** (Look panel) now blurs the map behind what the player looks at, not the gun.
- New **Blur with distance** switch (Look panel, Camera): the gun and what's near stay sharp and the map blurs more the further away it is, like a depth pass in After Effects. Off by default; **Blur distance** sets how far.
- New **View field of view** (Viewmodel panel) for the shots through the players' eyes: 90 is CS2's. The gun's own is now called **Gun field of view**.

## CS2's own poses on new demos

Demos from CS2's latest updates store the players' poses in a new format that OFAST couldn't read, so their players got OFAST's own animation only. OFAST reads it again: on round 14 of FaZe vs Falcons on de_anubis, 70 % of the players' frames now use CS2's own pose.

## Also

- Final renders of CS2's look are no longer washed out.
- The Studio shows a progress bar while it sends a round (also on its taskbar button) and plays a sound when it's done.
- Shot cameras in tight places: the camera behind the killer rises over their head instead of pressing against them, and fixed cameras keep away from the players.
- Known issue: in final renders, the shade under some ledges and lintels looks darker and bluer than in Unreal's viewport. It is being looked at.

Checked on CS2 1.41.9.5 (build 2000940).
