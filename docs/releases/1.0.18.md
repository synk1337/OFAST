Deaths: bodies no longer spin as they fall, and they fold and lie still about as in CS2. Also a fix for Unreal stopping with a graphics card error while loading a map.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.18_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

## Deaths

- Bodies spun half a turn or a full turn as they fell. CS2's demos do not record where a bullet landed, and OFAST took that missing point for the map's centre, so the hit's push twisted the body around it. The push is now applied at the body part that was hit, so the body falls away from the shot.
- 1.0.17 halved the hit's push against those spins; it is back to its earlier strength.
- Bodies toppled stiffly, like planks, and some landed kneeling upright. They now fold and drop within about a quarter of a second of CS2's.
- A body that has come down no longer keeps a hand creeping or twitching: it lies still once it has barely moved for a second.
- Compared with CS2's own playback of the kills of one round on Mirage, filmed from the same places at the same moments. A body that slides after a kill still slides as far as in CS2.

Rounds already sent keep their old version: send a round again to get the fixes.

## Unreal

- Unreal sometimes stopped with a graphics card error in the first seconds after a map's level loaded: in 10 of 24 tries in our tests on the realistic look of Mirage, and earlier on CS2's look of Inferno and Anubis. A setting in OFAST's Unreal project now avoids it: no error in 24 tries. The picture is the same; a frame takes about 0.7 ms longer.
- The update puts the setting in your project. If Unreal is open while you update, close it and open it again.

## Known issues

- CS2's bodies crumple at the knees from the first instants; OFAST's start folding a little later.
- A player killed while crouching straightens their legs a little before the body falls.
- With the players layer, the players are lit without the light bounced from the walls around them: in a dark place they come out too dark.
- A matchmaking or tournament demo recorded since CS2's update of November 21 has not been tried yet: if one does not open, please report it on GitHub.

Checked on CS2 1.42.0.0 (build 2000948).
