Support for CS2 1.41.9.8: the Studio shows it green again. And through a player's eyes, the view comes back down after a shot as it does in CS2.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.14_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

## CS2 1.41.9.8

- CS2's update of November 17 changed its engine, repacked its files and added a map, Cobblestone. OFAST exports and imports rounds as before: the reference round gives the same files as on 1.41.9.7, byte for byte. Only the Studio's check of your CS2 version changed: 1.0.13 and earlier flag this build as not yet checked.
- Demos of the new build: two rounds of bots recorded offline on 1.41.9.8 export without a warning. A matchmaking or tournament demo recorded since the update has not been tried yet: if one does not open, please report it on GitHub.
- Cobblestone: the map exports, without its distant scenery (the 3D skybox), which this CS2 build does not load either. It has not been tried in Unreal or with a demo yet.

## The view after a shot

- Seen through the player's eyes, the view went up with each shot and stayed up until the player's next sharp move; in CS2 it is back down within 0.4 second. 1.0.13 and earlier listed this as "the gun kicks up more after each shot than in CS2": it was the view, not the gun. Views were 1 to 3 degrees off the demo after shots, and players up to 7 cm from their place.
- Rounds already sent keep their old movement: send a round again to get the fix.

## Known issues

- With the players layer, the players are lit without the light bounced from the walls around them: in a dark place they come out too dark.

Checked on CS2 1.41.9.8 (build 2000946).
