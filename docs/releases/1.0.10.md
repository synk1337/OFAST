Scopes and sights as in CS2, shotguns that load shell by shell, reloads back in first person on new demos, each gun's own muzzle flash, bullet holes that open with doors, and counter-strafes played as CS2 plays them.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.10_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

**Send your rounds again** to see the changes. The maps don't need importing again.

## Scopes

- In shots through a player's eyes, the AWP, SSG 08, SCAR-20 and G3SG1 zoom in as in CS2 (twice for a second zoom), the gun disappears and CS2's black scope is drawn over the view. The other players' views stay as they are.
- New **Scope overlay** switch (Look panel, Camera), on by default: turn it off to put your own scope over the footage in editing. The zoom stays.
- The AUG and SG 553 zoom in too, and their sight comes up to the eye: the world around the lens is blurred and the green dot sits in its middle, as in CS2.

## Reloads

- Shotguns: the Nova, XM1014 and Sawed-Off load one shell for each shell that goes in, and a reload cut short by a shot stops there. They used to play one shell and freeze.
- On demos from CS2's latest updates, guns reload through the player's eyes again, with the same moves as in CS2: the reload used to stop after a quarter of a second, because CS2 now records the rest of a reload in a new way.

## Muzzle flashes

Every gun fires its own flash, the one CS2 gives it: the AWP's long flame, the rifles', the machine guns', the SMGs' and the pistols', the Desert Eagle's big one, and the silenced guns' small puff. Seen through the player's eyes the pistols' flashes are now real fireballs, where they were barely visible.

## Doors

A bullet hole in a door now moves with the door when it opens, instead of staying in the air where the door was.

## Players' legs

- Counter-strafes: players plant their feet when CS2 does (on the same tick for three plants in four) and hold the plant as long as CS2 does, where they used to plant about five ticks late and play the whole plant through quick left-right taps. Their walk then carries on in step with CS2's.
- More of CS2's own animations are recognised (reloads among them), so more of the players' frames use CS2's own pose, on new and older demos: on the round OFAST is checked on, 94 % of them instead of 83 %, and the demo's bullets hit within an inch of the bodies 42 times in 51 instead of 32.

## Known issues

- Seen through the player's eyes, the gun kicks up more after each shot than in CS2.
- In final renders, the shade under some ledges and lintels looks darker and bluer than in Unreal's viewport.

Checked on CS2 1.41.9.6 (build 2000942).
