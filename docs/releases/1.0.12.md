Support for CS2 1.41.9.7: the Studio shows it green again.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.12_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay; nothing needs sending again.

## CS2 1.41.9.7

CS2's update of November 11 changed its engine, re-saved character models and changed the C4's display. OFAST exports and imports rounds as before: the same files, the same players and the same frames in Unreal. Only the Studio's check of your CS2 version changed: 1.0.11 and earlier flag this build as not yet checked.

Checked on CS2 1.41.9.7 (build 2000943).
