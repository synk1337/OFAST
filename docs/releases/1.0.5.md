Rounds in Unreal now run until right before the respawn, the match's last round too, and skip the freeze time's wait.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.5_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay. Send a round again to get the changes in it.

## The whole round, without the wait

- A round sent to Unreal runs until right before the players respawn for the next one, with the seconds after the round is decided in it. The match's last round used to stop on the final kill; it now runs on for those seconds too, so the kill plays out (the players stand still then, as in CS2).
- It starts 3 seconds before the freeze time ends, instead of at its start: the players only stand at their spawn before that, and 3 seconds leave room for a cinematic as the round begins.
- **Render**'s lengths (whole round, around the kills) follow the shorter start.

Your cinematics stay at the same moments of the round when you send it again.

Checked on CS2 1.41.9.4 (build 2000939).
