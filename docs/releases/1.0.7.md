Maps closer to CS2's picture: lamps that glow, rooms lit as far as CS2 lights them, see-through glass and real steel.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.7_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

**Send a round** to get the new maps: the first round you send on each map imports that map again (a few minutes, once per map and look).

## Lamps glow

Bulbs, fluorescent tubes, screens and stained glass light up as in CS2, where every map lamp looked switched off.

## Rooms lit as far as CS2 lights them

The lights CS2 shines into rooms and tunnels through a frame (its barn lights) now reach as far as in CS2 and fade at the same edges. de_dust2's lower tunnel is now as bright as in CS2; de_inferno's library and de_nuke's lobby, much darker before, are a lot closer to it.

## Glass and steel

- Windows and panes are see-through, frosted or tinted as CS2 draws them; they were white walls (de_nuke's decontamination room, de_mirage's shop windows, de_inferno's lanterns). Dusty glass still holds back the light behind it, as in CS2.
- Steel is metal: sinks, gratings, corrugated ceilings and trims were exported as white paint until now.

## Also

- Metals in the shade still look darker than in CS2 (de_nuke's corrugated ceilings): Unreal lights their reflections differently. It is on the roadmap.

Checked on CS2 1.41.9.5 (build 2000940).
