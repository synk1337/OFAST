The snipers' muzzle flash, remade against CS2's own playback: through the player's eyes it now shows while the gun is in hand and is gone within a few ticks, instead of floating where the gun had been after a quick switch.

**Download** `OFAST-Studio_1.0.19_x64-setup.exe` below and run it over your current version, or press **Update** in the Studio's bar (1.0.3 and later). The maps and rounds you already sent stay.

## AWP and the other snipers

- Through the player's eyes the flash was a quarter of CS2's size, hidden behind the end of the barrel while the AWP was in hand. It is now as big as CS2's: CS2 draws the snipers' flash at full size in first person, where the other guns' are made smaller.
- It came too late. It started small and was at its brightest about 5 ticks after the shot, when CS2's is already gone, so after a quick switch to the knife it showed floating in the air. It now grows and fades as CS2's does: a big fireball on the shot's tick and the next, fading over two more, then gone.
- As in CS2, the flash is not cut when the weapon is switched: after a quick switch it plays out its few ticks at the crosshair.
- Compared tick by tick with CS2's playback of two AWP shots of a round on Mirage, one kept in hand and one quick-switched: within a tick of CS2's on both.
- Same for the SSG 08 (same flash). The SCAR-20 and G3SG1 use the same flash too and got the same change; not compared on a shot of theirs.

Rounds already sent keep their old version: send a round again to get the new flash.

## Known issues

- The snipers' flash seen from another player (third person) was not compared with CS2: it is now a full fireball at the barrel for 2 – 3 ticks.
- CS2's bodies crumple at the knees from the first instants; OFAST's start folding a little later.
- A player killed while crouching straightens their legs a little before the body falls.
- With the players layer, the players are lit without the light bounced from the walls around them: in a dark place they come out too dark.
- A matchmaking or tournament demo recorded since CS2's update of November 21 has not been tried yet: if one does not open, please report it on GitHub.

Checked on CS2 1.42.0.0 (build 2000948).
